HOW TO GET GOOGLE RATINGS FOR A LIST OF BUSINESSES
==================================================

This tool takes a Google Sheet of businesses (like an Apollo export) and finds
each business's Google star rating, number of reviews, and Google listing link.

It works on Mac and Windows. You don't need to type any commands.


BEFORE YOU START: get the folder onto your computer
---------------------------------------------------
You need the whole folder, not just one file.

From GitHub: on the project page, click the green "Code" button, then
"Download ZIP".

Then unzip it:
   Mac: double-click the .zip.
   Windows: right-click the .zip, choose "Extract All", then click "Extract".
Put the folder somewhere easy to find, like the Desktop. (It may be called
something like "google-reviews-scraper-main". The name doesn't matter.)


STEP 1. Let the tool read your sheet
------------------------------------
1. Open your Google Sheet.
2. Click the "Share" button (top right).
3. Under "General access", choose "Anyone with the link", set to "Viewer".
4. Click "Done".


STEP 2. Copy the link to the tab you want
-----------------------------------------
1. Click the tab at the bottom of the sheet that you want (for example "Dental").
2. Click the address bar at the top of your browser and copy the whole link.

   The sheet needs a column with the business name (like "Company Name").
   A street address, city or state column makes the results more accurate.


STEP 3. Run the tool
--------------------
1. Open the folder.
2. Double-click the file for your computer:
      Mac:      "Get Google Reviews (Mac)"
      Windows:  "Get Google Reviews (Windows)"
   A window with text opens.

   If you see a warning instead:
      Mac ("can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify"): click "Done",
         open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
         "Open Anyway" next to the file's name. (On older Macs: right-click
         the file, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.)
      Windows ("Windows protected your PC"): click "More info", then
         "Run anyway".

3. Paste the link you copied and press Enter.
   (Windows: right-click inside the window to paste, or press Ctrl+V.)
4. Type a name for the results (for example: Dental) and press Enter.
5. Wait. You'll see one line per business as it goes.
   - Roughly 100 businesses take 5 to 10 minutes.
   - Keep the window open and the laptop open. Plug in the charger for big lists.
   - Sometimes it says "Google showed a captcha. Pausing 10 min". That's normal.
     Leave it alone; it carries on by itself.

When it's done, a folder window opens with your results file selected
(inside the "Results" folder, named after what you typed).

To do another tab, paste the next link. To finish, press Enter on an empty line.


THE VERY FIRST TIME on a computer
---------------------------------
The first run spends a few minutes setting itself up. It only does this once.

Mac: if it asks to install "command line developer tools", click Install, wait
until it finishes, then double-click "Get Google Reviews (Mac)" again.

Windows: the computer needs Python (free). If the window says Python isn't
installed:
   1. Go to https://www.python.org/downloads/ and click the yellow Download button.
   2. Open the file you downloaded.
   3. On the first screen, TICK THE BOX "Add python.exe to PATH" (important),
      then click "Install Now".
   4. When it says "Setup was successful", close it and double-click
      "Get Google Reviews (Windows)" again.


STEP 4. Put the results into Google Sheets
------------------------------------------
1. Open your Google Sheet.
2. Click File > Import > Upload, and choose the results file.
3. Under "Import location", choose "Insert new sheet(s)".
4. Click "Import data".

A new tab appears with all your original columns plus:

   Google Rating         the star rating, for example 4.8
   Google Reviews        how many reviews, for example 1,120
   Google Business Link  click it to open the business on Google Maps
   Match Check           how sure the match is (see below)
   Google Listing Name   the business name as Google shows it
   Google Address        the address as Google shows it

(You can also just double-click the results file to open it in Excel or Numbers.)


WHAT "MATCH CHECK" MEANS
------------------------
   match                          The name and town agree. Safe to use.
   match (no reviews on Google)   The listing exists but has no reviews yet.
   check: Google shows "..."      Google found a business with a different name
                                  or in a different town. Click the Google
                                  Business Link to see if it's really the same
                                  business before you use the numbers.
   not found on Google Maps       No listing was found.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
"Couldn't download the sheet"
   The sheet isn't shared. Do Step 1 again.

It stopped with "Google is blocking lookups for now"
   Nothing is lost. Wait about an hour, run it again with the SAME link and
   the SAME name, and it carries on from where it stopped.

The laptop went to sleep or the window was closed
   Run it again with the same link and the same name. It skips everything it
   already looked up.

It found the wrong business for some rows
   Those rows say "check" in Match Check. Adding a street address or city
   column to your sheet makes the matches more accurate.

"Setup didn't finish"
   Check the internet connection and run it again.

Mac: double-clicking does nothing, or it opens in a text editor
   Open the Terminal app, type  bash  followed by a space, drag the
   "Get Google Reviews (Mac)" file into the Terminal window, and press Enter.
